About

Meet the inspector behind Bosch Home Inspections.

Michael Bosch

Owner & Inspector

Michael came to home inspection the practical way: by renovating homes himself. Over the past nine years, he and his wife Teresa have renovated more than 70 properties across Northern Virginia and Maryland through their family real estate business. He knows firsthand how homes are put together, where they tend to fail, and what it takes to fix them — and he brings that builder's eye to every inspection.

Before real estate, Michael spent more than 20 years as a trumpet player with the U.S. Air Force Band — a career built on preparation and precision, habits that now show up in every inspection and report.

Clients work directly with Michael from first call to final report — no call centers, no handoffs. Questions after the inspection are always welcome.
