Meet the inspector behind Bosch Home Inspections.

Michael Bosch

Owner & Inspector

Michael Bosch founded Bosch Home Inspections to bring careful, methodical evaluations to home buyers and sellers across Northern Virginia. A home is most people's largest purchase, and Michael's approach reflects that: every inspection is unhurried, systematic, and documented in a report you can actually use.

Clients work directly with Michael from first call to final report — no call centers, no handoffs. Questions after the inspection are always welcome.
