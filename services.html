Standard Home Inspection

A comprehensive visual evaluation of the home's structure and major systems for buyers, sellers, and homeowners. The inspection covers:

  • Roof, gutters, and drainage
  • Exterior siding, trim, and grading
  • Foundation and structural components
  • Attic, insulation, and ventilation
  • Electrical system and panels
  • Plumbing supply, drains, and water heater
  • Heating and cooling systems
  • Interior walls, ceilings, floors, and windows
  • Doors, stairs, and railings
  • Garage and built-in appliances

Inspection Types

Buyer's Inspection

A full pre-purchase evaluation so you understand the home's condition before closing, so you can negotiate from an informed position.

Pre-Listing Inspection

For sellers: identify issues before listing so they can be repaired or disclosed up front, keeping the sale on track.
